            How it works

            Ten questions a day, the same for everyone, new at 9:00 AM Eastern. For each one, name an NBA player who fits. You get one pick and 20 seconds per question, and every question counts: there’s no skipping.

            A right answer scores 1 to 100. Among all the players who fit, the most obvious scores 1 and the least obvious 100. How obvious a player is comes from his career (awards, games, longevity) and from how memorable his link to the question is: a star’s short, forgotten stint can score well. A wrong name or running out of time scores 0. Leaving or refreshing during a question counts as running out of time.

            Your final score is the average of the ten, out of 100. Team names are as they were that season: the Seattle SuperSonics are not the Thunder.
