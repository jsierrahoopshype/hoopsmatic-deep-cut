          How it works
          
  
          Five prompts a day, the same for everyone, new at 9:00 AM Eastern. For each one, name an NBA player who fits. One right answer finishes the round.
          
  
          Each answer scores 10 to 100. Among all the players who fit, the most obvious scores 10 and the least obvious 100. How obvious a player is comes from his career (awards, games, longevity) and from how memorable his link to the prompt is: a star’s short, forgotten stint can score well. Scores are fixed and final the moment you answer.
          
  
          You have 3 mistakes for the whole run. A third mistake ends it; skipped and unplayed rounds score 0. Team names are as they were that season: the Seattle SuperSonics are not the Thunder.
