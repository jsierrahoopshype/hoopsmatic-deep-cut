          How it works
          

          Every day at 9:00 AM ET there is one prompt. Name a player who fits it. You get 3 tries: a pick that doesn’t fit uses one, and 3 misses score 0.
          

          Your instant score (0-100) comes from how obscure your pick is in the data: fame first, then games and minutes. The next morning your final score also counts how few people picked the same player, and the crowd counts for more as more people play.
          

          Team names are as they were in those seasons: the Seattle SuperSonics are not the Thunder.
